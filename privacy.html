🍌 yo, BANANAS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

yo, BANANAS is made to be fun, fair, and private. This page explains exactly what information the game stores and why. Short version: we store your game progress so it follows your account, and nothing else. We never sell data, we show no ads, and we do not track you.

What we collect

If you create an account, we store:

  • Your username and password. The username you pick is shown to other players (leaderboards, races, battles, trades). Passwords are stored securely (hashed) by our database provider — we can't read them.
  • Your game progress: levels beaten, deaths, banana coins, powerups, characters and custom-drawn monkeys, trophies, titles, achievements, and settings — so they sync between your devices.
  • Multiplayer activity: race times, battle wins/losses, and trades, which power the public leaderboards and matchmaking.
  • A random device identifier, used only to enforce bans against players who break the rules.
  • Crash reports (technical data about the app crashing, no personal content), used only to fix bugs.

You can also play offline without an account — then your progress stays only on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • No real names, birthdays, photos, or contact lists
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers — there are no ads at all
  • No tracking across other apps or websites

Tip: pick a username that is not your real name.

Where your data lives

Game data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, protected by industry-standard security. Only the game and its developers can access it, and developer tools are used solely to run live events, fix mistakes (like restoring lost items), and moderate cheating.

Kids' privacy

The game is designed to be safe for all ages: no ads, no free-text chat with strangers, no purchases of real-world goods, and the only personal information ever asked for is a made-up username. We comply with children's privacy laws (including COPPA). If you are a parent and want your child's account deleted, email us — it will be removed completely.

Deleting your data

Email rohanmago99@gmail.com with your username and we will permanently delete your account and all its data within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
