Support & Help

📧 Contact us

Questions, bug reports, lost accounts, or trade problems — email rohanmago99@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as fast as we can (usually within a few days).

🎮 Quick help

How do I play? Hold to charge, release to fling your monkey at the banana. Use the LEAN buttons mid-air to curve. Don't touch the queen bee.

My progress didn't sync. Make sure you're logged into your account (👤 button on the home screen) and connected to the internet — progress, coins, trophies, and your zoo save to your account automatically.

I lost my password. There is no password reset — that's why the game tells you to write it down! Email us and we'll see what we can do.

Something was taken from my account by mistake. Email us with your username — our support tools can restore coins, characters, trophies, and skins.

Locked buttons that say COMING SOON? Those are future updates. When we release one, you'll see the unlock happen live in the game.
